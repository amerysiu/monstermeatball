## The World (rulebook page 1, ~150 words)

Long ago, the world was filled with ordinary ingredients.

Then came **The Great Flavorfall** — a strange fog that swept the wilderness, and every animal and plant it touched became something else. Some grew enormous. Others changed entirely: fire in their blood, crystals beneath their skin, petals where horns should be.

People discovered these monsters weren't just dangerous. They were **edible** — and their bodies held three precious essences: **Meat**, **Aroma**, and **Seasoning**. But how a monster is hunted changes what it yields. A careful hand preserves quality. A rushed one loses it. A brutal one can unlock power — at a cost.

This gave rise to a new profession: the **Monster Chef**.

Every ten years, the kingdom holds the **Grand Meatball Festival**. This year, the Fog is spreading again. Ancient ruins are surfacing. Somewhere at the heart of the wilderness lies the legendary **Crucible of Flavor** — and whoever reaches it first may shape what the Fog becomes next.

## Ending Table (resolve at game end — pure lookup, no reading required mid-game)

At the end of the game, check each player's **Kill Log** (tally kept during play: Clean / Rushed / Brutal / Tamed counts) against the table below. Apply the *first* row that matches, top to bottom.

| Condition | Ending | One-line flavor |
|---|---|---|
| Tamed 3+ monsters this game | 🕊️ **The Guardian Chef** | You didn't conquer the wilderness. You understood it. |
| Brutal kills = majority of all kills | 🔥 **The Wild Chef** | The Crucible stirs. Power has its price. |
| Clean kills = majority, Brutal kills = 0 | 🌟 **The Master Chef** | Precision and patience — the Festival has found its winner. |
| None of the above / mixed approach | 📖 **The Forgotten Recipe** | No single chef understood it all. Perhaps that was never the point. |

*(Ties between multiple players hitting the same ending are fine — they share the flavor result; Gourmet Points still determine the actual game winner. The ending is a narrative epilogue, not a scoring mechanic.)*

## Full Character Bios

### 🔪 Butcher Bob — *The Cleaver*
Enormous, cheerful, and surprisingly gentle. Bob grew up in his family's butcher shop and learned that hunting is easy — respecting what comes after is the hard part. He believes strength solves everything, until the wilderness teaches him that raw power destroys as much as it provides. By the Crucible, he's learned to control his strength instead of just increasing it.

### 🏹 Tracker Tessa — *The Sharpshot*
Precise, self-reliant, and a little too confident in her own read of the world. Tessa studies wind, footprints, and feeding patterns rather than trusting brute force. Her journey is learning that the Fog doesn't behave the way careful observation predicts — and that waiting for certainty isn't always mastery.

### 🪤 Trapper Tim — *The Snarer*
Clever, cautious, and quietly convinced that ten minutes of preparation beats ten seconds of combat. Tim would rather understand a monster than fight it. His arc is discovering that excessive caution can be its own kind of danger — and learning to trust his instincts when there's no time left to plan.

### ⚔️ Hunter Hank — *The Spearman*
The youngest, loudest, and most eager to prove himself. Hank believes courage solves everything a plan can't. Early wins make him overconfident, until he meets something bravery alone can't defeat. By the end, he's the one the others look to — not because he's fearless, but because he's learned when *not* to fight.

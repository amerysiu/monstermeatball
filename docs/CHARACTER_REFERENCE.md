# MONSTER MEATBALL — Character Reference Sheet
*(One card-back equivalent per character. Combines Storyboard bio with Session 2 mechanics.)*

---

## 🔪 BUTCHER BOB — *The Cleaver*
**Arc:** Power → Restraint

> "A good hunter kills once. A good butcher wastes nothing."

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Base ATK | **3** (highest in the game) |
| Max Stamina | 3 |
| Starting Equipment | Cleaver (equipped) |

**Passive — "Heavy Hand"**
- Brutal kills cost **no Stamina** (force comes naturally to him)
- Clean/Careful kills cost **+1 extra Stamina** (restraint doesn't)

**Signature Power — "Perfect Cut"** *(once per game)*
Extract from 2 edges of the same monster in one Act.

**Table Reminder:** Bob wants to Hunt often and Brutal — but every Clean kill he attempts is expensive. Players who lean into his strength early and his restraint late are playing his arc correctly.

## 🏹 TRACKER TESSA — *The Sharpshot*
**Arc:** Precision → Adaptability

> "The perfect shot is the one that leaves the ingredients untouched."

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Base ATK | 2 |
| Max Stamina | **4** (highest — she conserves energy) |
| Starting Equipment | +1 Reroll Token (in addition to standard 2) |

**Passive — "Efficient Hunter"**
- May extract monsters directly at Ruins, skipping the shop trip entirely
- **Trade-off:** must resolve any Fog card face-up *before* choosing her Risk Posture that turn — she can't bluff through uncertainty the way others can

**Signature Power — "Ambush Hunter"** *(once per game)*
Hunt 2 monsters in a single Act action.

**Table Reminder:** Tessa is efficient with routes (skips shops) but punished by randomness (Fog). She wants a controlled game — Fog-heavy rounds are her personal challenge.

## 🪤 TRAPPER TIM — *The Snarer*
**Arc:** Caution → Courage

> "Why fight the monster when you can convince it to defeat itself?"

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Base ATK | **1** (lowest in the game) |
| Max Stamina | 3 |
| Starting Equipment | Trap (equipped) |

**Passive — "Snarer's Patience"**
- Facedown Ruins hunted **immediately upon reveal** are always Clean-eligible, regardless of current Stamina

**Signature Power — "Preservation Expert"** *(once per game)*
Ignore all spoilage checks for the rest of this round.

**Table Reminder:** Tim is weak in a straight fight but strong when he acts fast on fresh reveals. His low ATK means late-game Tier III monsters are a genuine threat to him — that's his "courage" moment built into the stat line.

---

## ⚔️ HUNTER HANK — *The Spearman*
**Arc:** Courage → Wisdom

> "If it's bigger than me, it's probably worth hunting."

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Base ATK | 2 |
| Max Stamina | 3 |
| Starting Equipment | +1 bonus AP token (usable once, any round) |

**Passive — "Steady Nerve"**
- Once per round, may Hunt without spending Stamina

**Signature Power — "Fearless Feast"** *(once per game)*
Complete a dish without paying one required ingredient type.

**Optional Expert Rule — "Impulsive"** *(Round 1 only)*
If Hank is adjacent to an unrevealed Ruin at the start of his turn, he must spend his first AP exploring it. This restriction disappears from Round 2 onward — mechanically dramatizing his growth.

**Table Reminder:** Hank has no built-in weakness (balanced pick for new players) — his "wisdom" arc is expressed through the optional Impulsive rule rather than a permanent stat cost, so Expert-tier tables get to feel his growth; Basic/Standard tables just get a clean, easy-to-teach all-rounder.
